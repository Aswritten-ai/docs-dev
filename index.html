Welcome to aswritten

Installable expertise from humans you trust. Cited by AI.

Try it right now — install aswritten’s own perspective. aswritten publishes the public projection of its own knowledge graph: positioning, product thinking, architecture, and the reasoning behind them. Two steps.

1. Connect aswritten to your AI (one time):

  • Claude Desktop — no code. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name it aswritten, server URL https://api.aswritten.ai/mcp, press Connect, and sign in when the browser opens.

  • Claude Code — one line, added to your user profile so it works in every project:

    claude mcp add --scope user --transport http aswritten https://api.aswritten.ai/mcp
  • Other MCP tools (GitHub Copilot, Cursor) — add an MCP server with that same URL. Details in Quick Start.

2. Ask for the perspective — in plain words, in any chat:

“Load the aswritten perspective with the ID customer-architecture-demo-enterprise-c1cb, then tell me how aswritten thinks about pricing.”

Your AI answers grounded in the company’s actual recorded decisions, with citations. Prefer to browse first? The same perspective is a website: the public perspective navigator.

That’s the demo — your AI reading aswritten’s mind. The product is your AI reading yours, built from conversations you’re already having: your call transcripts and your AI working sessions. Build your own — the first version takes an afternoon, not a quarter.

What is this?

Aswritten.ai turns expertise from the humans you trust into a citeable perspective for AI. Your AI tool — Claude (Code or Desktop), GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or any other MCP-compatible client — loads the perspective and answers grounded in actual organizational knowledge instead of guessing. Every answer cites where it came from.

MCP is the Model Context Protocol — the standard way AI tools connect to external data sources and capabilities. It’s how aswritten plugs into Claude, GitHub Copilot, and other AI clients.

Start here — which one are you?

“My team invited me to a perspective”

Everything is already set up. Log in with the email from your invite — no card, nothing to pick. Follow Quick Start: I was invited, then ask your AI whatever you’d ask a senior person on the team.

“I use AI every day, and every session starts with re-explaining myself”

Build your own perspective. Your AI loads it at session start, works from your accumulated decisions and methodology, and cites you back to yourself. If you record your calls, you already have the source material — start from your transcripts on the Expert plan.

“I need my team to work from my expertise without me in the room”

Build your perspective, then share it. Your team’s AI works from how you think — and citations show you exactly what influenced each output, so you review instead of re-explaining. Start with Quick Start: build your own on the Team plan, then add your teammates.

“I have a book, a course, or a methodology worth distributing”

We turn your existing material into a living perspective your audience installs into their AI — cited to your actual work, growing as you add to it. This starts with a conversation, not a setup flow: email scarlet@aswritten.ai to schedule an interview. See Pricing.

“My organization keeps re-explaining itself — to new hires, to clients, to its own AI agents”

The Organization plan builds a private catalogue of role and department perspectives, created with your senior people. Deploy a perspective as an agent — chat or SMS — so colleagues, customers, or partners can query it without installing anything. Email scarlet@aswritten.ai to scope an engagement. See Pricing.

Try a question

Once a perspective is loaded, ask your AI something only your team would know:

  • “What’s our position on [a topic relevant to your team]?”
  • “What did we decide about [a recent decision area]?”
  • “What’s still unsettled in [a domain you work in]?”

If the AI cites specific people and memories instead of giving generic advice, your perspective is working. If something seems wrong or missing, add to it with remember.

Need help?

Type “I need help” in any chat with aswritten connected. Your message goes directly to our team. Or email perspective@aswritten.ai.
