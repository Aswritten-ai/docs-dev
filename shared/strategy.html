1. Thesis

The why underneath

What should AI be steered by? The answer this company exists to give: our conversations with each other and our own thoughts — not constant prompting, not the average of the internet as told by the AI labs. Whose voices are in the intelligence is the stake; aswritten is an amplifier of human diversity, not a uniformity machine. The personal register of the same why (Aug 8, verbatim): “I want to live more in the world and less in the digital world. And I want AI to act on my behalf in the digital world, instead of me having to dissociate from my body and the space that I’m in constantly to do so.” The standing promise — reachable without being interruptible — is that sentence at product scale. (Aug 8 memos; extraction in flight.)

The two-level product thesis

The meta-goal is synthetic identity — a co-evolving, graph-sourced, bounded relational AI: reactions drawn from what is and isn’t in the organization’s graph, advancing at the integration pace, the model interpreting rather than providing. Relationship, not mimicry; a state machine, not AGI; no false intimacy. (Consolidating in the product domain: the 5Ws+H canonical model, the expression graph, the integration dial. See 2026-06-05-1-synthetic-identity-meta-goal-and-graph-sourced-reactions.md.)

The applied framing — what we sell today — is organizational decision-capture: aswritten models who said what and what they made of it, makes that perspective queryable and citable by AI, and deploys it where the organization works — including as agents on real channels, where the standing promise is the proxy one: reachable without being interruptible. Individual, role, team, organization are configurations of one architecture, not separate products.

The product-vision staircase: org decision-capture (selling now) → amplified roles and advisors (already running internally — the named-perspective advisor sub-agents) → synthetic coworkers → a marketplace of identities (deferred, long-term). Each stage is funded by the previous stage’s validated demand. V1’s marketplace framing was early-arc material presented as the present; it is re-staged, not abandoned.

The felt articulation (Aug 8): the watcher and the reaction. The meta-goal’s “graph-sourced reactions” now has its consumer-legible register. A watcher witnesses the conversations you’re already having — calls, memos, email, commits, coding sessions — into your own repo, unattested by default; the graph delta triggers the reaction, and the reaction is the creativity: the write-up after the call, the prototype waiting when you hang up. Making memory-saving a goal of the conversation is the design flaw, not the feature; consent relocates from the moment of capture to the moment of sharing and attestation (“you agree, and that’s the grant”). Two structural consequences: the deployed-representative concept demotes to one aswritten-managed agent that moves across perspectives and channels (a Teams deployment is a channel of it, never a fleet of bespoke agents); and the prompt surface sheds workflow enforcement — with a watcher there is no save-a-memory goal to impose on sessions, so the Claude-side integration reduces to MCP tool descriptions: pull the perspective with a focus before tasks; attest before anything critical. Source memos: 2026-08-09-1-watcher-passive-witness-across-surfaces.md + 2026-08-09-1-creativity-aswritten-reaction-is-the-creativity.md (dev; extraction in flight); working synthesis: docs/design/artifact-archive/2026-08-08-clean-vision-view.html (dev).

“Synthetic identity” is internal language. External materials keep the accessible register — “AI that carries your organization’s actual judgment and reacts from it” — until naming is deliberately settled. Naming in progress (2026-06-24): the working coinage is Synthesized Individual Intelligence (SII) — a deliberate reclaim of “AGI” (you own the *particular, the installed perspective, not the lab’s general substrate), with “individually owned AGI” as the public hook. Unratified; product-doc-v2.md §1 carries the full frame.*

The business thesis: calibrated growth

Per 2026-06-05-1-calibrated-growth-staircase-discipline.md (committed):

  • Team sizing and pricing are calibrated to validated demand, not venture timelines. No bloating ahead of application; no abstract product without delivery; no team growth before customer pipeline justifies it. Growth drawn from current success, iteratively validated through actual purchase, not venture approval.
  • Profitability at each team configuration is the math check, not the destination. Each round is sized so the team it funds could reach profitability on a calibrated customer ramp. Raises are strategic acceleration, never survival.
  • The SEL is the capital-structure expression of this discipline; profit-share retainers are the same alignment applied to senior hires. The alignment principle scales.

The two distribution engines

  1. Top-down enterprise — the Escher motion: warm introduction, sale-as-delivery, land small, expand inside the account (seats → Org tier → deployment options).
  2. Bottom-up distribution — individuals from the founder’s network try it, integrations make trials seed instantly, advocates emerge, teams convert, and the consulting wedge bridges trial-interest to paid organizational engagement. (Design: docs/internal/2026-06-09-distribution-engine-handoff.md.)

The founder’s network of operators, founders, and engineers is the single largest underused asset; the second engine exists to use it.

Resolved (2026-08-09): the B2B-vs-B2C axis is dissolved, not chosen on. Scarlet, verbatim: “B2C and B2B isn’t a super helpful distinction here… this organization isn’t trying to pick one and stay in the lane… There are different values in our process for different folks at different parts of that level. We will find the right niche, but I think it’s going to be on a different axis” — one that may trim pieces off either side, or both. The continuum principle (one use case, differentiated by how many people write to and consume the perspective) stays load-bearing; the June-24 founder-fit case folds in as strategy, not a market pick — enthusiasm, ownership alignment, in-person geography, and mission enactment (task-599) are validation criteria, and in founder-led sales conviction is the mechanism. Operationally: parallel validation workstreams across the continuum — the enterprise (Escher), the practitioner and their web, the creative individual — with the enterprise motion taking clear precedence now. The niche-defining axis is an open decision (Section 10), expected to emerge from what the parallel validation shows.

2. The unmovable goal and the five paths

How goals anchor this strategy

Strategy here works by reverse-sequencing: name the unmovable goal, name the movable goals near it, sequence dependencies back from there in order of required / tertiary / nice-to-have. Everything else is pushed to the margins by construction.

The current unmovable goal

~September 1, 2026 — the founder’s father’s funding ends. By then something must have happened that materially extends runway.

The goal is not binary. One $25k check extends runway by months. A YC acceptance changes the funding picture entirely. The Escher MSA converting locks in $63k of year-one contract value. Multiple small wins compound; any single significant win substantially extends the line. The posture: five paths in parallel, with different dependency shapes and cycle times, so no single path’s failure is fatal.

The next unmovable goal beyond

The $3-4M institutional round, 6-12 months after bridge close — sized for the full team (Luke, Laura, Daniel, Jess, an AI/NLP-leaning engineer) across the 36-48-month swing-plus-second-swing window. Pursued from strength, at the strategic moment, never from survival pressure. Some near-term work (advisor formalization, case studies, content presence) seeds this goal; it is informed by but not the anchor of this document.

Path A — Friends & family bridge ($500k target, explicitly incremental)

Why: long-standing relationships convert to runway through an instrument designed for them — SAFE + SEL, $8-12M cap, no discount, checks from $25k. The structure embodies the thesis: aligned capital, optionality preserved, no treadmill.

Provides: incremental runway (any check counts); a base of aligned early investors; proof the relationship-led shape works.

Why: converts the pilot into $4k/mo base recurring (expected $6-9k with deployment options; $63k year-one), the strongest single proof point across every other path.

Why: repeatability is the foundational proof for the institutional round and is not established by one customer. Customer #2 sourced beyond the immediate family also defuses the concentration critique directly.

Approaching the portfolio before that proof exists burns the warmest pipeline. Until the gate opens: pilot networking continues through the summer, the healthcare-practice conversation stays live, and the near-term motion is smaller teams onto Team-tier trials within ~a month — trials feed the pilot pipeline and the bottom-up engine simultaneously. Full pilot pushes land in fall, leveraging angel/YC sales networks. Onboarding readiness (transcript-integration seeding) lowers friction for all of it.

Interactions: integration work (distribution handoff Workstream 1) shortens C2 onboarding; C1’s case material qualifies C2 prospects; a C2 land transforms B and the institutional narrative.

Path C3 — Consulting wedge

Why: a different sales motion — bounded $5-10k engagements, faster cycles, lower entry commitment — that productizes the founder’s existing consulting strength. About the client’s organization, not adoption of the tool. Generates revenue inside the goal window, case-study material, and a pipeline of future Org-tier customers. A paid engagement from outside the family network is the cheapest honest repeatability signal available.

  • July — the angel-first push begins; YC submitted by July 26.
  • August — YC decision (Aug 28); family follow-on conversations against the angel anchor.
  • Fall — pilots and Team-trial conversions, leveraging angel/YC sales networks; institutional-round warm leads built.

4. Engagement and relationship strategy

A targeting brief, not a calendar. The founder writes what she wants, when she wants; this section is the information consulted when deciding who to write for and what would help.

The principle

Relationships that aren’t yet sales are real strategic infrastructure. The ask comes after engagement — for family, after weeks of following along; for advisors, after the consent conversation; for consulting prospects, after the value framing has landed. The brief, the pitch, the ask are end-or-mid-sequence artifacts, never openers. One person, one primary ask (the relationship-role map below).

Publish liberally (decided June 11). The IP posture: give the full tour of the castle, withholding at most the security room — “and even that, you could probably walk someone in and show them the camera feeds. Why not?” The moat is the transformation pipeline and the accumulated graph, not the ideas; holding content back costs the warming arc more than disclosure could cost the moat.

5. Customer strategy

Three channels

  1. Expand current — Escher: MSA conversion, deployment options as compliance needs surface (the contract is itself the upsell instrument), then the PE-portfolio doorway (~12 right-shaped companies) and the university path.
  2. Repeat the motion — second and third pilots on the Escher shape: land small, sale-as-delivery, expand in-account.
  3. Consulting-first entry — the C3 wedge: bounded engagement → installed perspective → continuation decision.

Who (and why them)

Mid-market enterprise software vendors (50-500) and successful professional practices (5-30, medical/legal/financial) — organizations that run on recurring, high-stakes decision-making whose reasoning evaporates. The data-ownership architecture (machinery, not storage; ZDR-capable) is what makes the regulated practice segment addressable at all. Deliberately not Fortune 500.

The math check (not a forecast)

Bridge-team burn ≈ $52k/mo. Average enterprise customer ≈ $7.5k/mo (base + deployment options, modeled). ≈7 enterprise customers = bridge-team profitability. Current state: Escher $680/mo (17 Team seats) converting toward $4-9k; total MRR $761 including one Expert seat (Taylor — genuine heavy daily use, deliberately excluded from traction claims). The check’s job is keeping team sizing honest, not predicting the quarter.

Escher contingency (the keeps-you-up-at-night runbook)

Concentration here is correlated risk — every path leans on Escher. Naming the scenarios is the mitigation for the founder’s sleep as much as for the business:

  • Churn before MSA close (worst): bridge resizes down ($300-400k framing: “shorter bridge to a customer-2-anchored close”); consulting wedge accelerates to primary revenue; customer #2 search becomes the top priority; the brief reframes traction around pipeline; institutional timing slips a quarter. Survivable by design.
  • Churn during bridge, before customer #2: bridge cash is in hand; no forced panic. Exit interview becomes a learning artifact; “validated and exited” is discounted but real traction.
  • Churn after customers 2-3: ordinary SaaS churn; replace and document.

Standing mitigations: multi-touchpoint Escher engagement (CTO, chief architect, eng manager, PE board — all already engaged; keep the cadence); the motion documented in transferable form (not solely in the founder’s head); leading indicators watched (ARB ingestion rate, CTO touch frequency, legal-review pace, seat engagement); revenue diversification via C3.

8. Product strategy

Briefing altitude: what gates what. The architecture lives in the product domain (PR #242 consolidation); build mechanics live in tasks.

The v1/v2 line, honestly drawn. Today the product captures discourse with attribution and conviction, compiles and cites perspectives, deploys agents on real channels (the reachable without being interruptible surface), and supports the Escher engagement in production. The v2 architecture — reconciliation, conviction trajectory, projections, graph-sourced reactions — is where the full value proposition the materials gesture at actually lands. v2 is load-bearing, not deferrable: the consulting deliverable’s depth, customer #2’s onboarding quality, and diligence-resistance (YC interviews, technical evaluators) all draw on it. Implementation is paced by customer demand and real capacity — threaded through, never parked.

The solo partner is the join (June 11). The v2 build leads with the solo partner — one human at full dimensional depth, the integration dial — which is simultaneously the fundraise demo (its spine: path-dependent, trajectory-grounded reactions — “you’ve been softening on this for three weeks”) and the proof-of-concept for the organizational extent. The governing principle makes this one bet, not two: dimensions are properties of speech; boundaries are properties of audiences — nothing built solo is thrown away; the org case adds audience machinery (clearance, federation, role topology) as an upgrade path for existing revenue. And the solo build’s mechanics are required for the shipping product regardless: collapse/expand (task-265, the lynchpin — today’s OOM/token failures), temporality (surfaced by Escher directly), durable annotation-grounded conviction (extraction brittleness). “R&D line = product line.” Source of truth: docs/internal/product-doc-v2.md §7 (product domain).

Customer engagements are the product tests. Escher MSA delivery exercises deployment options; customer #2 onboarding tests the repeatable motion; consulting engagements test the analytics deliverable; investor demos test legibility. Feature investment follows what these surface — calibrated growth applied to the roadmap.

The integration play: aswritten sits above the transcription layer (Fireflies first — existing work to build on; Otter, Granola next). Integrations solve trial cold-start (seed a starter perspective from the user’s own recent meetings), shorten C2 onboarding, and speed C3 analytics. Two modes coexist by design: automated ingest → review for operational volume; conversational shaping for strategic work. (The integration dial, in product-domain terms.) The watcher generalizes this beyond transcription (Aug 8): coding sessions, email, texts, and commits become watchable surfaces on the already-ratified write path (merge-is-publication, unattested-by-default, continuous tranches) — new connectors, not new semantics; memory-per-commit gives repos co-located conceptual commentary mapping one-to-one to what was done. The four flow archetypes (individuation · conversation · attestation-and-reliance · representation) are the product-doc’s flow spine — absorption into product-doc-v2.md pending the source-memo extraction.

Claims discipline: external materials may sell only what the current product plus founder-judgment-in-the-loop can deliver — and as of June 11 the v1/v2 gap is no longer phrased around: it is the pitch. The brief sells the bridge explicitly — production traction on v1 and the solo-partner PoC in hand as proof; what the round buys is the PoC’s application across the enterprise extent, shipping to the paying customer as upgrades as each piece proves. Diligence-precise phrasing, ready: “the organizational product is in production on the current architecture; the new architecture ships to it as upgrades as each piece is proven.” No pause-revenue-to-build-the-future tax.
