Sales Partnership & Commission Structure

Status: Active — applies to all sales partnerships Date: March 19, 2026 Context: The “Expertise on Demand” positioning from Strategy Document V1 defines what sales partners sell and how. This document covers the general sales partnership model, commission structure, and the two-step sales path. Clay Harper is the current potential sales partner.

What We’re Selling

The product positioning is “install expertise from humans you trust, cited by AI.” Sales partners sell expert perspective creation1 — turning someone’s book, course, or consulting methodology into a living AI perspective, then distributing it through a marketplace.2

The perspective creation service IS the value proposition.3 It’s not a setup fee — it’s the thing we do.

Provenance (What’s Changed) — 3/3 claims grounded, 100%. The positioning shift, Clay’s new pitch direction, and the perspective-creation-as-value reframe all trace to the Mar 19 strategy and sales sessions.

The Two-Step Sales Path

Step 1: Expert perspective creation → Team tier

Who to approach: Individuals and small businesses with a single distributable asset — a book, a course, a consulting methodology, a body of professional practice.

The pitch: “Your book is a snapshot. We’ll turn it into a living AI perspective.9 Your expertise becomes installable, citable, and generates revenue through the marketplace.”

What happens:

  1. Sales partner identifies the prospect and the asset (book, course, etc.)
  2. Sales call doubles as onboarding — the conversation becomes the first memory
  3. We convert their asset into a full perspective (professional perspective creation service)
  4. They’re on the Team tier ($400/month) with a live perspective

Revenue events:

  • Creation fee (upfront — for the professional perspective creation and build)
  • Team subscription ($400/month recurring)
  • Marketplace revenue share (percentage of perspective sales, ongoing)

Why this works for sales partners: Bounded, clear value, repeatable. Every expert with a book or course is a prospect.10 The perspective creation is a defined engagement with a tangible deliverable. You’re not selling software — you’re selling “let me turn your expertise into something AI can distribute.”

Step 2: Organization upsell

Who: Teams who want multiple perspectives, or larger organizations that want an internal expertise marketplace (a “catalogue”).

The upsell trigger: “You’ve published one perspective. Now imagine every role in your organization has one.”11

What happens:

  1. Sales partner identifies organizations where multiple roles or domains could benefit
  2. Scarlet joins for the Organization-tier sales call
  3. We do a scoped $15K engagement: interview key people, create role perspectives, populate the internal catalogue

Revenue events:

  • Organization setup fee ($15K upfront)
  • Organization subscription ($4K/month recurring)
  • On-prem add-ons as applicable

The hooks at this level: Compliance control, data sovereignty, private catalogue, role-based expertise that persists across people.

Provenance (Clay’s Path) — 3/3 key claims grounded, 100%. The Team pitch, prospect identification, and Organization upsell trigger all trace to the Mar 19-20 sales strategy sessions. Commission model remains an open question — no decision reached.

Commission Options

Option A: One-time on conversion/setup fees

Event Partner cut Example
Team perspective creation fee 50% If fee is $2K → Clay gets $1K
Organization setup ($15K) 30-50% $4.5K-$7.5K per deal
  • Pro: Immediate cash. Simple. Motivates volume.
  • Con: No ongoing incentive after the perspective creation.

Option B: Recurring on subscription revenue

Tier Monthly cut Annual
Team ($400/month) 15-20% ($60-80/mo) $720-960/year per account
Organization ($4K/month) 10-15% ($400-600/mo) $4.8K-7.2K/year per account
  • Pro: Compounds over time. Aligned with retention.
  • Con: Slow money upfront. Takes ~6 months on Team to equal a one-time $500.

Option C: Hybrid (recommended to discuss)

  • One-time commission on conversion/setup fees (immediate reward)
  • Smaller recurring cut (5-10%) on subscription revenue (ongoing incentive)

  • Marketplace revenue share commission TBD (if Clay’s prospect generates marketplace revenue)

  • Pro: Immediate + ongoing. Clay benefits from both the perspective creation and the long-term relationship.
  • Con: More complex to track.

Equity Trigger Terms

  • Setup/perspective creation fees go to PnD to offset bare-bones salary

Upgrade Path Narratives (Updated)

Value-driven upgrade (I want more)

Free → Expert: “I want to add my own knowledge, not just use others’ perspectives.” Expert → Team: “I want to publish my expertise / collaborate with my team.” Team → Organization: “I want my own internal catalogue of organizational expertise.”

Requirements-driven upgrade (I need this)

Free → Expert: “I need introspect — the LLM query tools.” Expert → Team: “I need my own GitHub, multiple perspectives, or a team.” Team → Organization: “Compliance says on-prem” or “We need a private catalogue” or “We need an MSA/SOW.”

The two cases that validate both drivers

Escher (Mike Sackton): Enterprise consultancy, 150 people. Arrives at Organization for value (full perspective creation of organizational expertise for QA/interns/CEO) AND requirements (GDPR, European employees, cross-repo compliance). Mike: “$400/month isn’t real money.”13

Peter Caron (Gotham Acupuncture): Course creator and practitioner. Arrives at Team for value (package course series for students and prescribing practitioners).14 Potential Organization-tier if practice grows and Jordan’s new business requires a private catalogue.
