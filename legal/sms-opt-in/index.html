aswritten.ai SMS Opt-In Evidence

This page documents the SMS opt-in flow for aswritten.ai for the purposes of toll-free verification and ongoing compliance review. It is intended as a reference for Twilio reviewers, carriers, and compliance partners.

For the public-facing program description, see the SMS Program page. For the messaging terms, see Terms §5. For privacy, see the Privacy Policy.

Operator: aswritten.ai is operated by Synthetic Identity Co., a Delaware corporation. Contact: legal@aswritten.ai.

Summary

aswritten.ai recipients opt in to receive SMS messages through one of two documented paths:

  1. Web form (VIA_WEBSITE). A user submits a multi-step signup form at app.youform.com/forms/fjsk7iwt, which collects explicit SMS consent before any phone number is added to the messaging system.
  2. Business-provided number (VIA_OTHER). A business that has deployed an AI agent on aswritten.ai gives the agent’s phone number to one of its existing clients, after collecting that client’s SMS consent at onboarding under the business’s own privacy and consent practices. The client texts the number to initiate a conversation.

In both paths, recipients can reply STOP at any time to opt out, START to re-subscribe, or HELP for help.

aswritten.ai does not share recipient phone numbers with third parties for marketing purposes.

Path 1: Web form opt-in (VIA_WEBSITE)

The signup form lives at https://app.youform.com/forms/fjsk7iwt and is linked from the SMS Program page on docs.aswritten.ai. It is a three-step flow.

Step 1 — Program description and contact info

The visitor first sees a description of what aswritten.ai is, what they will receive if they sign up, and an explicit reference to SMS consent on the next step. They then enter their first name, last name, email, and phone number.

Step 1 copy (verbatim):

aswritten.ai

AI agents grounded in real expertise, reachable by text.

Sign up to demo our agent and get updates on aswritten.ai. If you’re an expert or a business interested in deploying your own agent, leave your phone number and we’ll be in touch.

On the next step we’ll confirm your consent to receive SMS messages from aswritten.ai. Reply STOP to opt out anytime.

Step 2 — Explicit SMS consent (required Yes/No selection)

Before the visitor’s phone number is submitted, they are presented with a dedicated SMS consent screen. The screen presents the full SMS disclosure and a required Yes/No selection: “Yes, text me.” or “No.” The visitor must affirmatively select an option and submit. Selecting “No.” ends the form without enrolling the visitor in SMS messages. Selecting “Yes, text me.” records their consent and proceeds to confirmation.

Step 2 copy (verbatim):

SMS Consent

By clicking yes and submitting this form, I consent to receive SMS messages from aswritten.ai (operated by Synthetic Identity Co.) at the phone number I provided. Messages may include responses from a deployed AI agent, conversation continuations, and account notifications. Message frequency varies based on my conversation activity. Message and data rates may apply.

I can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

aswritten.ai will not share my phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. See Privacy Policy and Terms.

Step 3 — Confirmation

After consent is recorded, the visitor sees a confirmation screen and the form closes. The first SMS message they receive includes opt-out instructions per CTIA guidelines.

Path 2: Business-provided number opt-in

In the B2B2C path, a business that has deployed an AI agent on aswritten.ai provides the agent’s phone number directly to one of its existing clients. Consent for SMS messaging is collected by the deploying business at the time of client onboarding, under the business’s own privacy and consent practices.

aswritten.ai’s responsibilities in this path:

  • The deploying business agrees, as part of using aswritten.ai, that it will collect explicit, written or recorded SMS consent from each end user before that user’s phone number is associated with a deployed agent.
  • aswritten.ai’s Terms §5 (SMS Messaging Terms) require this consent collection.
  • aswritten.ai will, on request from a carrier or compliance reviewer, work with the deploying business to produce that business’s consent record for any specific recipient.
  • An end user can reply STOP at any time, regardless of how their number was added; this immediately removes the number from the active messaging list.

This path is appropriate for the use case where a recipient already has an established relationship with the deploying business (e.g., they are a client of the consulting firm, professional practice, or expert who has deployed the agent) and have given consent through that relationship.

STOP, START, and HELP behavior

  • STOP — On receipt of any STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT keyword, the recipient receives a confirmation message and is removed from the active messaging list. No further messages are sent.
  • START — A recipient who has previously opted out can re-subscribe by replying START.
  • HELP — On receipt of HELP, the recipient receives a message including a description of the program, contact information (support@aswritten.ai), and STOP instructions.

These behaviors are implemented at the messaging-infrastructure level (Twilio) and do not depend on the deployed agent.
