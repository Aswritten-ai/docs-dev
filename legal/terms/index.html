2. Service Description

The Service enables Subscribers to construct, maintain, and consult a “Perspective” — the structured representation of an organization’s expertise — and to deploy AI agents grounded in that Perspective. Components include:

  • Perspective authoring tools. The MCP-based tool surface (perspective, cite, introspect, remember, review) plus a web admin
  • Perspective consultation. Any MCP-compatible AI tool can consult an installed Perspective and receive responses cited back to underlying memories
  • Perspective sharing. Subscribers may share a Perspective with others by share ID
  • Agent deployment (Team, Organization, and MSA-covered tiers). A Perspective may be deployed as an AI agent reachable on SMS, email, Slack, and other supported channels, with operator observability, draft mode, and human-in-the-loop override
  • Engagement services (Organization and MSA-covered tiers, performed by PnD). Consultant-led perspective construction, channel provisioning, operator training, and related setup and ongoing services

Specific features available depend on the plan or applicable SOW.
