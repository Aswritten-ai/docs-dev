Pricing

aswritten lets you install expertise into your AI and verify where every claim comes from. Three tiers, each defined by what you can do.

Expert — $81/month

Build your own perspective. Your AI gets smarter over time.

  • Install and use shared perspectives, with citations on every claim
  • Your own perspective (managed for you — no GitHub account needed)
  • Save your knowledge with remember — your perspective grows with every session
  • Remove what’s no longer true with forget — clean up inherited knowledge or make big shifts
  • Ask what’s missing with introspect — see gaps in your knowledge
  • Review and refine before knowledge enters your perspective
  • Share your perspective with others — they cite your words
  • Optionally connect GitHub for multiple perspectives and version control access

How you get here: We interview you, and a starter perspective is waiting in your account. Add your own materials with remember and it grows every session.

Team — $400/month

Collaborate on shared perspectives. Your team cites your expertise.

  • Everything in Expert
  • Connect your own GitHub account — you own your data
  • Manage multiple perspectives
  • Up to 10 team members collaborate on the same perspective
  • Higher usage limits
  • Publish to the marketplace

How you get here: Your Expert perspective transfers to your own GitHub account. You add your team. Everyone works from the same perspective.

Organization — $4,000/month + engagement

Your own private catalogue of organizational expertise.

  • Everything in Team
  • Private internal catalogue — role perspectives, department knowledge, onboarding frameworks
  • We interview your senior people and build the perspectives for you
  • Deploy your perspective as an agent (chat or SMS) — colleagues, customers, or partners query it without installing MCP themselves; admins see the conversations and can step in
  • Custom ontology development for your domain
  • On-prem deployment for compliance (HIPAA, GDPR, data sovereignty)
  • Unlimited seats
  • Dedicated support

How you get here: Contact us. We scope an engagement, interview your experts, and deliver working perspectives.

How upgrades work

Getting started (Expert): “I want to build my own perspective.” We provision a repo for you — no GitHub needed. Your interview perspective imports automatically as the foundation for your own.

Expert → Expert + GitHub: “I want multiple perspectives or version control.” Connect your GitHub account. Write to any repo where the app is installed. Your managed repo is still there — now you can also work across your own repos.

Expert → Team: “I want my team to collaborate.” Your repo transfers to your own GitHub account. You add team members. Everyone reads and writes to the same perspective — live, not snapshots.

Team → Organization: “I want a private catalogue for my org.” We scope an engagement, interview your experts, and build role perspectives.

Data ownership

On every tier, your perspective is yours. Expert repos are managed by aswritten but your data is portable. On Team and above, you own the repo directly in your own GitHub account.

The perspectives you install from others are cited expertise — you see the expert’s claims and reasoning, not their raw source material. The creator’s proprietary knowledge stays protected.
