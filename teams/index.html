aswritten in Microsoft Teams

aswritten reads the transcript of a Teams meeting after it ends, writes a memory from it into your organization’s perspective, and asks the people the meeting concerns to review what was written about them. No bot joins the call, and aswritten never receives audio or video: it reads the transcript Teams produces.

In a meeting

When aswritten is on a meeting, a card in the meeting chat says what will happen:

  • Transcription needs to be on for aswritten to read this meeting. aswritten reads only transcripts. Turn transcription on with More (…) → Record and transcribe.
  • aswritten is reading this meeting. When the meeting ends, aswritten reads the transcript and writes a memory that everyone who was there can review. The transcript itself stays in Teams.
  • This meeting is private. It won’t be read.

If the meeting is set to record and transcribe automatically, transcription starts on its own.

Keeping a meeting private

Press Disable aswritten for this meeting on the card, or type @aswritten private in the meeting chat. The meeting won’t be read. Read this meeting after all undoes it.

Only people in the organizer’s organization can change whether a meeting is read. Guests still get a link to review anything attributed to them.

If a meeting has already been read, marking it private stops anything further; the memory already written can be removed from the perspective.

If someone removes aswritten from a meeting, it is never added back to that meeting.

Asking aswritten

Mention aswritten in the meeting chat with your question, or ask it in your own chat with aswritten (no mention needed there). Answers come from your organization’s perspective, with a citation for each claim. In a meeting chat you can also type @aswritten status or @aswritten help.

In a chat or Team channel aswritten has been added to, it reads the messages, so a memory can quote what was said there with the speaker and the time. It answers only when it is mentioned.

After the meeting

  • aswritten writes a memory from the transcript into your organization’s perspective, in the repository your organization owns.
  • The people the meeting concerns are asked to review what was attributed to them: by email from review@aswritten.ai, in the meeting chat, or in their own chat with aswritten. Replying to the email replies to aswritten.
  • If the meeting ends and no transcript arrives, the card says Nothing was captured, and the organizer is told by email.

See Review Mode for how review works.

How aswritten gets onto a meeting

  1. Added to one meeting. In the meeting or its chat, open Apps and add aswritten. You choose which perspective the meeting’s memories go to, and which of your Teams the meeting belongs to, if any.
  2. Added to a Team. aswritten reads the Team’s channel meetings. Meetings that members schedule in their own calendars are not covered this way; aswritten has to be added to those.
  3. Connected once, by an organizer. An organizer who connects their Microsoft account has aswritten added to every Teams meeting they organize, as soon as the meeting is scheduled, and the meeting is set to record and transcribe automatically. Type stop covering my meetings in your chat with aswritten to turn this off, and cover my calendar to turn it back on.

Adding aswritten to your own Teams gives you a chat with aswritten. It does not put aswritten on any meeting.

For IT administrators

Installing and updating the app

aswritten is a custom app in your organization’s Teams app catalog: Teams admin center → Teams apps → Manage apps → Upload, using the package aswritten provides. An app permission policy decides who can use it.

When a new version asks for new permissions, upload the new package the same way (open aswritten in Manage apps, then Upload file). Until it is uploaded, aswritten cannot be added to new meetings.

Tenant settings it needs

  • Meeting transcription access via API on. When it is off, aswritten cannot read any transcript.
  • Include speaker attribution on. When it is off, transcripts arrive without speaker names, and aswritten attributes no statement to any person.
  • Transcription enabled in the meeting policy for the people whose meetings aswritten reads.
  • Resource-specific consent not set to DisabledForAllApps for chats or Teams. Under that setting nobody can add aswritten to a meeting, chat or Team.

Teams PowerShell sets the first two:

Set-CsTeamsMeetingConfiguration -Identity Global -EnableGraphTranscriptAccess $true -EnableAttributedTranscripts $true

Permissions

Granted where the app is added, by the person who adds it to a meeting or chat, or by a Team owner. Each reaches only that meeting, chat or Team.

Permission Granted for Used for
OnlineMeeting.ReadBasic.Chat One meeting The meeting’s name, schedule, organizer, and start and end
OnlineMeetingTranscript.Read.Chat One meeting The transcript the memory is written from
ChatMember.Read.Chat One meeting or chat Who was invited, so review reaches them
ChatMessage.Read.Chat One meeting or chat Quoting what was said in the chat
ChannelMeeting.ReadBasic.Group One Team The same as the first row, for channel meetings
ChannelMeetingTranscript.Read.Group One Team Transcripts of the Team’s channel meetings
TeamMember.Read.Group One Team The Team’s members
ChannelMessage.Read.Group One Team Quoting what was said in the Team’s channels

Granted by each person who signs in, acting only as that person: sign-in and their own name and email; their calendar’s meeting entries (never invitation bodies or attachments), so aswritten can be added to the meetings they organize; transcripts of their own meetings and of calls they transcribed; adding aswritten to their meetings’ chats and pinning its tab there; and reading the names and members of their chats and Teams, never chat messages.

Not requested: organization-wide reading of chats or channels, recordings of any kind, mail, files, SharePoint, the directory, or any live control of a meeting. The one change aswritten makes to a meeting is the record-and-transcribe-automatically setting, on meetings organized by someone who connected.

Data and privacy

  • aswritten never receives audio or video.
  • Transcript text is turned into a memory committed to your organization’s repository. aswritten’s servers hold the transcript only while it is processed.
  • Model calls run under zero-data-retention routing. See Trust & Security.
